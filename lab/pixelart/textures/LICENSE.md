# Stone scans

Diffuse and displacement maps from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0
(public domain), downscaled to 384 px so one texel covers roughly a third of a game pixel at the
scale the kit renders. Sources: concrete_wall_008, dark_rock_02, lichen_rock, mossy_rock,
rock_pitted_mossy, rock_surface, worn_rock_natural_01.
