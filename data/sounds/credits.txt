licence. Sounds released under Creative Commons 0 carry no attribution
requirement and are listed here for traceability only; the remaining entries
are listed because their licence requires the author to be credited.

Licences were taken from the sound pages linked below. When adding new
material, check the licence on the source page before shipping it.
